# Characters

`soldier.glb` and `enemy.glb` are made by `tools/blender/make_soldier.py` (presets `soldier` and `enemy`) from:

- A MakeHuman character built with [MPFB](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/) and the
  [MakeHuman system assets](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html)
  (CC0 1.0): the base body, the `young_caucasian_male` skin, `low-poly` eyes, `eyebrow001`, `eyelashes01`,
  `short02` hair, `shoes03`, and `male_casualsuit01` (soldier) or `male_casualsuit05` (enemy), dyed by the script.
  Characters exported from MakeHuman are CC0. The enemy's helmet is modeled by the script.
- The skeleton and skin weights of NVIDIA's SOMA base character (`soma_base_skel_minimal_no_root.usd`, CC BY 4.0,
  see `../../../motion/assets/soma/`), which the characters are posed to and skinned from.
