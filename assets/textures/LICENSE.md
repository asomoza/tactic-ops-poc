# Textures

From [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use, modify and ship, no attribution
required.

| Folder | Source | Used for | Size in the game |
|---|---|---|---|
| `concrete_floor_worn_001` | https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001 (2k) | Ground | 3 m |
| `concrete_block_wall` | https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_block_wall (1k) | Walls (full cover) | 2 m |
| `brick_wall_02` | https://polyhaven.com/a/brick_wall_02 (1k) | Low walls (half cover) | 2 m |

Each folder holds the JPG maps as downloaded, renamed: `diff.jpg` (base color), `nor_gl.jpg` (OpenGL normal map)
and `arm.jpg` (ambient occlusion, roughness, metalness).
